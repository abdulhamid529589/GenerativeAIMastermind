```

TOPIC: [Your topic]
```

### Glossary of LinkedIn Terms

**Algorithm:**
The system LinkedIn uses to determine what content appears in users' feeds.

**Connection:**
Someone you're directly connected to on LinkedIn (1st degree).

**Engagement:**
Interactions with your content (likes, comments, shares).

**Engagement Rate:**
Percentage of people who interact with your content out of total impressions.

**Featured Section:**
Highlighted content at top of your profile (posts, articles, media).

**Hashtag:**
Keyword preceded by # to categorize content. Use 3-5 per post.

**Impression:**
Number of times your content is displayed (whether clicked or not).

**InMail:**
Direct message to someone you're not connected with (requires Premium).

**Lead Gen Form:**
Native LinkedIn form for capturing leads without leaving platform.

**LinkedIn Premium:**
Paid subscription ($39.99-$99.99/month) with additional features.

**LinkedIn Live:**
Live video broadcasting feature (requires application/approval).

**Profile View:**
When someone views your LinkedIn profile.

**Reach:**
Total number of unique people who saw your content.

**Sales Navigator:**
Premium LinkedIn tool for sales professionals ($99.99-$149.99/month).

**SSI (Social Selling Index):**
LinkedIn's score (0-100) measuring your social selling effectiveness.

**1st Degree Connection:**
People you're directly connected to.

**2nd Degree Connection:**
People connected to your 1st degree connections.

**3rd Degree Connection:**
People connected to your 2nd degree connections.
